Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-25

This Privacy Policy explains how the WiFi QR Code Generator ("the Service") handles information. We built this tool to require as little data handling as possible.

1. What We Collect

The Service does not require an account, cookies, or any personal identifiers to function. When you generate a QR code, you submit a network name (SSID), an optional password, a security type, and a hidden-network flag. This data is:

  • Sent over HTTPS to the Service's backend only for the purpose of generating the QR code image.
  • Processed entirely in memory.
  • Never written to a database, log file, cache, or any persistent storage.
  • Never shared with third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers.
  • Discarded immediately once the QR code image is returned to your browser.

2. Generated QR Codes

The QR code image is generated on demand and returned directly to your browser as an image. It is not stored server-side. If you download it, the file lives only on your own device.

3. Infrastructure Logs

The underlying hosting infrastructure (e.g., AWS Lambda / API Gateway, or your own server) may generate standard operational logs (such as request timestamps, status codes, and IP addresses) for the purpose of operating and securing the Service. These infrastructure-level logs are governed by the hosting provider's own data handling practices and are not configured by this application to log network names or passwords. We do not access or use these logs to extract submitted network credentials.

4. Cookies & Tracking

The Service does not set cookies, use tracking pixels, or run third-party analytics scripts.

5. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Your Responsibility

Because QR codes encode your network credentials in plain form, treat generated QR codes and printed copies with the same care you would treat your WiFi password. Anyone who scans the code can join your network.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

This is a free, community-style tool provided without a dedicated support or compliance team. If you have concerns, do not enter sensitive credentials into the Service.
